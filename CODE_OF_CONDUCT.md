# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ORBIT to be a friendly place for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience, nationality, appearance, identity or beliefs.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to PS3 homebrew.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Stay on topic and assume good intentions.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks
- Sexualised content, threats, or sharing someone's private information
- Spam, piracy requests or links to pirated games
- Impersonating the project or other people

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules and may block people from the project. Report problems by contacting the maintainer through the GitHub profile [C2V4ZA](https://github.com/C2V4ZA).

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
